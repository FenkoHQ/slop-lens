Slop Lens settings

by Fenko

Show a slop reading on every page

Slop Lens can score each page as it loads, so the toolbar shows the reading without a click. That needs permission to read the pages you visit. Scoring stays on this device; nothing is sent anywhere.

Appearance

Use your device setting, or choose light or dark.

Toolbar

Show the slop number

Puts the reading on the toolbar badge, the way uBlock Origin shows its count. The badge takes the band colour.

Tint the icon by band

Colours the toolbar icon from green through to red as the slop reading climbs. Turn this off to keep the plain amber icon.

Preview

Scanning

Scan pages automatically

Scores every page as it finishes loading, so the toolbar is already coloured before you click. This needs permission to read the pages you visit, which the extension does not otherwise hold: without it it can only read a page when you click the icon. Nothing is sent anywhere either way.

Saved. Applies on the next scan.

Settings are stored on this device only, never synced.
