Most AI pilots do not fail because the technology is weak. They fail because the business case is. The companies getting it right are focused on three things: 1. Real workflows, not demos 2. Early governance 3. Measurable P&L impact Here is what we keep seeing across every industry we work in. Teams embark on a groundbreaking, comprehensive initiative. They delve into the landscape. Six months later there is a paradigm and no revenue. The teams that win start smaller. They pick one bottleneck. They measure it before and after. That is the whole method. At the end of the day, it is not the model that decides, it is the operating discipline around it.
